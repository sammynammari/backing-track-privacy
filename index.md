# Privacy Policy

*Backing Track Generator · Last updated October 22, 2026*

Backing Track Generator does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## What stays on your Mac
- **Your prompts and tracks.** Requests you type or say are turned into a track by a language model that runs on your Mac. Tracks, settings and recordings are saved only on your Mac, in places you choose.
- **Voice input.** When you use the microphone, speech is transcribed by Apple's speech recognition, on your Mac whenever your Mac supports it. Audio is never sent to us. If your Mac can't transcribe on-device, Apple's Speech service handles the request under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Network use
The app connects to the internet only to download its language model the first time it runs, from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). Like any web download, that server sees your IP address; nothing about you or your music is sent. See [Hugging Face's privacy policy](https://huggingface.co/privacy).

Links in the app to third-party sound plugins open in your browser and are covered by those sites' own policies.

## Children
The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
